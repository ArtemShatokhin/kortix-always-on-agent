An open-source sample week of customer feedback for the feedback agent in this Kortix project. Replace it with your own export and keep the same shape.

## 2026-10-13

- Acme Logistics, paying, Enterprise: the CSV export times out when the date range is more than 90 days. They run it monthly for the board pack and now do it in three slices.
- Brine Analytics, paying, Team: asked twice for a way to see which teammate ran which agent session last week. They are building this by hand from Slack.
- Nadia (trial, day 3): signed up to try the agent on her own repo, could not find where to put her provider key, left.

## 2026-10-14

- Acme Logistics, paying, Enterprise: follow-up on the export. Their finance team needs the same 12 columns every month, and the column picker does not remember the selection.
- Corvo Studio, paying, Pro: the export of a 30-day range works but takes four minutes, and they timed it.
- Theo (trial, day 1): asked whether this runs on his own machine or on Kortix's. No answer in the docs he could find.

## 2026-10-15

- Brine Analytics, paying, Team: second request for the session audit view, this time from their security lead, who wants it before a renewal in November.
- Kestrel Labs, paying, Team: the export drops rows with an empty `owner` field. They found it by comparing counts.
- Nadia (trial, day 5): came back, found the key setting, asked whether the free plan can use her own keys.

## 2026-10-16

- Corvo Studio, paying, Pro: asked for a scheduled export so nobody has to remember it.
- Kestrel Labs, paying, Team: confirmed the dropped rows, sent a file with 12 examples.
- Priya (trial, day 2): signed up after reading a comparison post, wants to know if audit logs are exportable.

## 2026-10-17

- Acme Logistics, paying, Enterprise: renewal conversation in three weeks. Their list: export reliability, remembered column sets, session audit view.
- Brine Analytics, paying, Team: the security lead asked for the same audit view in writing, with a date.
- Priya (trial, day 3): converted to Team.
